How it works

Four steps, one fixed price, no surprises for the business

  1. Readiness check

    Users, projects, apps, custom workflows, integrations and your deadline. One call, a written result.

  2. Plan

    Order of projects, app replacements, user mapping and a test migration. You get a fixed price.

  3. Watched migration

    Projects move in waves. Each wave is counted and checked; broken links and missing statuses are fixed on the spot.

  4. Sign-off

    A report per project: issues, attachments and links verified. Your teams keep working the next morning.
